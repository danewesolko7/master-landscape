Questions & Answers

Do commercial properties have an AB 1572 deadline?
Yes. Commercial, industrial and institutional properties must stop using potable water on nonfunctional turf by January 1, 2028.
Can you handle multiple properties?
Yes. We serve properties across four counties from three locations.
