Certified Irrigation Audits

An irrigation audit measures how evenly and efficiently your system waters, then lists the repairs and schedule changes that cut waste. Ours are performed by a Certified Irrigation Auditor on our staff.

Request a proposal →Submit an RFP

Certified Irrigation Auditor on staff. Audits support water rebate applications and AB 1572 planning.

What the Audit Checks

Four steps, in order

  • 01

    Inspect the system

    Walk every zone for leaks, broken or tilted heads, and pressure problems.

  • 02

    Measure the water

    Run a catch-can test to see how evenly each zone applies water.

  • 03

    Review the schedule

    Compare controller run times with plant needs, soil and season.

  • 04

    Report and prioritize

    A written list of repairs and schedule changes, ranked by water saved.

What you receive

A report your board can act on

  • Zone-by-zone findings with photos
  • How evenly each zone waters, measured
  • Recommended repairs, ranked by water saved
  • A revised watering schedule
  • Rebate programs the upgrades may qualify for

Rebates

Audits often come before rebates

Many water saving upgrades qualify for rebates. Some programs require approval before work begins, so we plan the audit, upgrades and paperwork together.

Questions & Answers

How long does an audit take?
It depends on the number of zones. We give you a schedule with the proposal. [Confirm typical duration.]
Do you fix what the audit finds?
Yes. Our irrigation crews can make every repair in the report, or you can use it to bid the work.
How often should a property be audited?
After major changes to the landscape or the system, and whenever water bills rise without an obvious cause.
