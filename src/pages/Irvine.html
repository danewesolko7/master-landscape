Local Conditions

Water, rebates and rules

  • 01

    Water supplier

    Irvine Ranch Water District (IRWD) serves most of Irvine. Check IRWD rules and rebates for your property.

  • 02

    Turf rebates

    Commercial turf replacement rebates through MWDOC require approval before any turf is removed.

  • 03

    AB 1572

    Nonfunctional turf deadlines: January 1, 2028 for commercial properties and January 1, 2029 for HOA common areas.
