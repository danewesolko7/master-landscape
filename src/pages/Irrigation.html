Questions & Answers

What property managers and boards ask us

What does an irrigation audit include?
A system inspection, a measured test of how evenly water is applied, a review of the controller schedule, and a written list of repairs and schedule changes. See certified irrigation audits.
Can you help us get water rebates?
Yes. Our water saving program covers the upgrades and the application. Some rebates require approval before any work starts, so ask us early.
