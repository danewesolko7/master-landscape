Design system

Refined brutalist

The tokens, type and components behind every page. Square corners, heavy rules, hard shadows and one gold accent.

Internal reference. Built from the live CSS, so it always matches the site. Not linked from the site and hidden from search engines.

Logo

01 · The M-and-laurel mark and the full lockup.

  • Mark: the M in a double ring with a laurel below.
  • Clear space: the ring's width on every side.
  • Minimum size: 32 px for the mark, 200 px for the lockup.
  • On dark: gold mark on ink (the favicon). Never white on gold.
  • Don't stretch, recolor, add shadows or set the name in another face.
  • Lockup name: Source Serif 4, bold plus italic.

Color

02 · Gold is for action and emphasis only.

Contrast pairs

WCAG AA needs 4.5 : 1 for body text and 3 : 1 for large text. Never put white text on gold: use ink on gold.

Layout

04 · Grid, spacing, rules and shadows.

  • 01

    Container

    1320 px max (--max); side padding --gutter grows from 24 px on phones to 32 px.

  • 02

    Hero grid

    Two-thirds text column, one-third side note, --space-11 gap; one column under 900 px.

  • 03

    Sections

    --space-9 top, --space-10 bottom; a 2 px ink rule between every section.

  • 04

    Grids

    grid--narrow, grid, grid--wide, grid--half: 5, 4, 3 and 2 across at 1440.

  • 05

    Rules

    2 px ink for structure, 1 px #B9C4BC hairlines inside lists.

  • 06

    Shadows

    Hard offset, no blur: 4 px buttons, 6 px cards, 8 px heroes and panels.

  • 07

    Corners

    Square everywhere. No radius.

  • 08

    Touch

    Every link and button is at least 44 × 44 px. Small links get .hit.

Spacing scale

Voice & imagery

13 · How the site sounds and what it shows.

  • 01

    Lead with proof

    Name the County, the cities and the credentials before adjectives.

  • 02

    Answer first

    Open every page with the direct answer to its question.

  • 03

    Plain and specific

    Short sentences. Numbers, places and property types.

  • 04

    Real photos only

    Their crews and properties, captioned with property type and city.

  • 05

    One accent

    Gold is for action and emphasis. One italic phrase per heading, at most.

  • 06

    Keep it simple

    No decorative extras. Square corners, heavy rules, hard shadows.
