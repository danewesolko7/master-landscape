Questions & Answers

When do HOA common areas have to meet AB 1572?
By January 1, 2029, potable water can no longer irrigate nonfunctional turf in HOA common areas.
Can you present to our board?
Yes. We walk the property and present proposals and budgets to boards.
Do you work with management companies?
Yes. Several of our current contracts are through property management companies.
