The short answer

A landscape budget has four parts: routine maintenance, irrigation and water, tree care, and planned improvements. Build it from a property walk, not last year's number, and phase big projects across years.

Line items to include

  • Maintenance: the contract amount for routine care.
  • Irrigation: repairs, upgrades and expected water costs.
  • Trees: the pruning cycle and arborist reports.
  • Improvements: renovations and turf conversions, phased.
  • Contingency: storm damage and emergencies.

Plan for water costs and AB 1572

Water is often the line that grows fastest. Audits and retrofits can lower it, and rebates can offset upgrade costs. HOA common areas must stop using potable water on nonfunctional turf by January 1, 2029, so conversion costs belong in the next few budgets.

Reserve study components

Long-life landscape items such as irrigation systems, hardscape and trees may belong in your reserve study rather than the operating budget. Ask your reserve specialist how they are treated.
