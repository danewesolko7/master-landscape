Questions & Answers

How much are commercial turf rebates?
Amounts depend on the program and change over time. Our rebate guide lists current MWDOC rates with a link to the program.
Does a rebate mean we comply with AB 1572?
No. Rebates and AB 1572 are separate. We plan for both.
