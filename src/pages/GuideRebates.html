The short answer

The Municipal Water District of Orange County (MWDOC) pays rebates for replacing turf on commercial and public agency properties. The key rule: don't remove any turf until your application is approved. Projects started before approval are not eligible.

How the rebate works

MWDOC lists base rates of $2 per square foot for commercial projects and $3 per square foot for public agency projects. Funding is limited, and rates can change, so check the program page before you budget.

Each project must include a sustainability feature and at least three plants per 100 square feet. Artificial turf is not eligible. An optional tree rebate adds $100 per tree, up to five trees.

Steps in order

  1. 1

    Apply

    Submit the application online before any work starts.

  2. 2

    Wait for approval

    Approval comes as a "Letter to Proceed." Nothing before it counts.

  3. 3

    Convert

    Remove turf, install planting, irrigation changes and the sustainability feature.

  4. 4

    Claim

    Submit completion documents to receive the rebate.

Rebates and AB 1572 are separate

A rebate does not confirm compliance with AB 1572, and the rebate program does not decide which turf is nonfunctional. Plan for both: the rebate pays for the work, the law sets the deadline. See our AB 1572 guide.
