Questions & Answers

What property managers and boards ask us

What types of properties do you maintain?
Municipal and public agency sites, HOA common areas, apartment communities, and commercial and industrial properties.
Who supervises the work on my property?
Every contract includes full-time on-staff supervision. Your supervisor is a Master Landscape employee, not a subcontractor.
Can you help us prepare a landscape budget?
Yes. We prepare annual landscape budgets for boards and property managers, including irrigation and tree care line items.
Which areas do you cover?
Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties, from our Westminster main office and facilities in Irvine and Chino.
