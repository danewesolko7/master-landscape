Local Conditions

Water, rebates and rules

  • 01

    Turf replacement rebates

    The Municipal Water District of Orange County (MWDOC) offers commercial turf replacement rebates. Approval is required before any turf is removed.

  • 02

    AB 1572 deadlines

    Potable water on nonfunctional turf ends January 1, 2028 for commercial properties and January 1, 2029 for HOA common areas.

  • 03

    Local water suppliers

    Rules and rebates vary by water supplier. City pages list the supplier for each city. [To confirm per city.]
