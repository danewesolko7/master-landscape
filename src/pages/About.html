About Master Landscape

Forty years of landscape in Southern California

A full service professional landscape maintenance and landscape construction company, serving Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties.

Led by President Robert Whitecotton, with over 30 years in the landscape business.

Our own supervisors, specialists and nursery

Our main office is in Westminster, with a satellite facility in Irvine and another in Chino that includes a wholesale nursery. Our locations and volume purchasing let us pass low material costs on to our customers.

Our client properties include municipal projects, homeowners associations, apartment complexes, and commercial and industrial developments.
