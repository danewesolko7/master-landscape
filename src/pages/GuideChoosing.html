The short answer

Choose a contractor on proof, not price alone: a valid license, insurance, references from properties like yours, on-site supervision and in-house specialists. Then compare proposals on the same scope.

What to check

  • License: verify it on the California State License Board site.
  • Insurance: ask for certificates naming your association or company.
  • References: properties of the same type and size.
  • Supervision: who is on site, and are they employees?
  • Specialists: arborists, irrigation auditors and a landscape architect on staff.

Compare proposals fairly

Give every bidder the same scope and map. Ask how extra work is priced. A low bid with a vague scope often costs more by year end.

Switching without disruption

Check your current contract's notice period. Schedule a walkthrough with the new contractor before the start date, and document the property's condition with photos.
