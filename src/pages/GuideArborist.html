The short answer

You usually need an arborist report when a city or HOA board must approve removing or heavily pruning a tree, when a tree may be a hazard, or when a dispute or insurance claim involves a tree. Rules vary by city.

Common reasons

  • Tree removal permits: many cities require a report for protected or large trees.
  • Board decisions: HOA boards often want a written assessment before voting.
  • Hazard concerns: leaning trees, cracked limbs, root damage.
  • Construction: protecting trees near new hardscape or utilities.

What a report contains

Tree species and size, health and structure, risk assessment, recommendations (prune, treat, monitor or remove) and photos. It should be prepared by an ISA Certified Arborist.
